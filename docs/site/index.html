Take-home · Multimodal Q&A extraction

Extracting questions and answers from textbook pages

A document-agnostic pipeline that reconstructs complete logical questions — with their sub-parts, marks, figures and answers — from scanned or born-digital chapters, and reports what it could not verify.

Two chapters, two different problems

The same pipeline handles both, choosing its reader per page. One chapter is image-only scans; the other carries a real text layer, and paying a vision model to re-read its pixels would be both worse and far more expensive.

What was measured, and what wasn't

Ground truth was transcribed by reading the source page images by hand. Gold built from the pipeline's own output would score 100 % by construction and mean nothing, so scope is stated rather than implied.

Marks at 100 % is the one worth pausing on. The self-assessment section prints the mark as a bare integer immediately after a Bloom tag — (Understand) 1. All eleven were read correctly, and the adjacent Bloom label was never mistaken for a weightage. Where a publisher prints no marks, the field stays null rather than being inferred.
Type accuracy of 65 % needs an honest reading. The question-type list mixes format types (short answer, long answer) with content types (conceptual, numerical, MCQ). Four of the seven disagreements are that ambiguity — gold followed the book's section headings, the system typed by content. Three are real errors, so genuine type error is nearer 15 %.

The measurement earned its keep

Building the gold set immediately exposed a truncation bug that every prior run had reported as a success. These are the defects the pipeline's own metrics could not see.

A case-study passage cut to nine words The answer-boundary rule matched the ordinary word answer mid-sentence, so “Read the given extract and answer the following questions…” was truncated to “Read the given extract and”. The boundary now has to be a real label at the start of a line.
Failed page reads cached as good data An empty or truncated response was stored in the cache, so every later run served the hole back and reported the document as reproducible. Three poisoned entries were on disk. Only validated results are cached now; failures go to a separate record with a reason.
A perfect text layer re-read by OCR The born-digital path forced full-page OCR, discarding exact characters and replacing them with a model's guess — “the slits are separated” came back as “sits are separatei”. Conversion also took four times longer.
A guard that disagreed with what it guarded The list of valid answer relationships existed twice — once in the associator, once in the evaluator. Adding one to the associator made every use of it report as a false association. Correct work looked wrong.

How a question gets rebuilt

A logical question is modelled as a span — from an offset in one block to an offset in another — rather than as a block. That makes joining fragments and splitting an over-bundled block the same operation: deciding where the boundary falls.

Joining

A question running across columns, pages or several paragraphs is followed transitively until a boundary. Structural, marker and grammatical signals are scored separately and stay inspectable; markers like Q1 or Ans. are evidence, never requirements, so a chapter that numbers nothing still works.

Splitting

A single block holding a stem and its parts is divided at the sub-part labels — but only when they form a genuine run, and never when they read as multiple-choice options. A solved example's worked solution is separated from the question rather than absorbed into it.

When the answer isn't there

The self-assessment section ends with a QR code linking to solutions hosted elsewhere. Those answers are genuinely absent from the chapter, and the system says so.

Across both chapters: zero false associations and zero generated answers. A positive match always requires structural evidence inside the question's own scope; the language model adjudicates between candidates and never writes an answer.

Known limitations

  • Coverage on the born-digital chapter measures 0.69 against 0.98 on the scanned one. Either figure regions are genuinely under-captured, or the threshold is calibrated for the other reader. The gate is left flagging rather than quietly relaxed.
  • Sub-question trees are the weakest axis at 71 %; two windows in the gold set expose parts that stay inline in their parent.
  • Answer counts vary by one or two between runs from model non-determinism, so a small delta is not evidence of a regression.
  • Question type needs the format/content ambiguity resolved before the number means anything.
